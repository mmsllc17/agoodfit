Before you host

Hosts keep A Good Fit safe and fun. As a host, you agree to:

Follow the Community Guidelines
Share an accurate route, pace and meeting spot
If it’s no-drop, nobody gets left behind
Cancel in the app if plans change

Community rides aren’t run by a club. Clubs can ask you to change a ride that uses their name.

Hosting rides is part of Plus

With A Good Fit Plus you can host community rides anytime. On the free plan you can still suggest this ride to GoodCo Bike Club, and if they approve it, you lead it.
